# Amendment 4 to the revision protocol (3 October 2026; the commit time is the record)

Recorded after the supervisor's review of the submission version, before any of the runs below. Every result is
reported whatever it shows.

1. **Interval calibration under other noise structures** (`scripts/run_calibration_stress.py`). Exact-rate records
   with the PVWatts response (the estimators' response is correct, so only the noise differs), ERA5 weather 2015–2022
   at the 20 weather sites, rate −1 %/yr, noise seeds 0 and 1, five noise modes: independent hourly noise of 0.5, 1
   and 3 %; day-to-day AR(1) noise (φ = 0.9, marginal σ = 1 %) plus 0.5 % hourly noise; heteroscedastic hourly noise
   σ = 1 % × √(1000/POA) (POA floored at 50 W/m²). Methods: the reference with RdTools' default interval, the same
   rate with a 30-day circular block bootstrap of its year-on-year slopes, and κ-HYDRA-D (five seeds and the
   leave-one-year-out jackknife). Outcomes per mode and method: coverage of the first-year truth with its Wilson
   interval, median width, interval score, site-level mean absolute error and bias.
2. **Physical references with an inverter model and with coefficients learned on the same record**
   (`scripts/run_inverter_reference.py`). (a) Measured records (18 main systems, AC power, fixed hour mask, the five
   injected rates): the reference with the PVWatts inverter part-load efficiency curve (nominal efficiency 0.96,
   inverter DC rating = array DC rating) applied to the PVWatts DC power with the technology-typical coefficient;
   and a physical model a × inverter(P_r G/1000 (1 + γ (T_c − 25)) (1 + k ln(G/1000))) with a, γ and the low-light
   coefficient k fitted by least squares to the first 12 months of the same record (fixed-mask hours, POA
   200–1200 W/m²), refitted on every injected record. Outcome: injected-change error against Eq. (2), locations
   weighted equally, and the change of the unmodified-record rate. (b) Exact-rate records of the 20 weather sites
   (PVWatts and thin-film-like generators, 240 records; ADR generator, 120 records; DC output, so no inverter curve):
   the fitted physical model against the reference with the typical coefficient on the same records; site-level mean
   absolute error against the first-year truth.
3. **Independent extension set of measured systems** (US DOE Regional Test Center baseline systems, Curran et al.
   2019, https://osf.io/yvzhk/, ODbL 1.0): inverter-level DC power of identical mono-Si strings with a ground POA
   pyranometer and module temperature, 1-min data, at Albuquerque NM (BSk), Williston VT (Dfb) and Cocoa FL (Cfa).
   The two Nevada inverters are excluded before any screen or rate: their site and start date coincide with those
   of PVDAQ 1423 (Henderson, NV), already in the main benchmark. Hourly aggregation and the Tier-S screen are those
   of the main benchmark, unchanged (hour kept with ≥ 90 % valid 1-min samples; clock convention against ERA5; power
   scale; night offset; POA saturation; ≥ 4 valid years; two-reference POA drift rule). Every system that passes
   enters; none is added or removed after a rate is computed. Methods, hyper-parameters and design are those of the
   main benchmark without any retuning: reference (typical coefficient), the seven two-stage models (five seeds),
   κ-HYDRA-D (full, with its jackknife at r = 0, and the no-physics and no-correction ablations) and κ-HYDRA-D-24,
   fixed hour mask, injected rates −0.25, −0.5, −1 and −2 %/yr. Results are reported as a separate extension set
   (`results/fixed_mask_rtc/`) and never pooled into the tables of the main benchmark. Pre-specified checks of the
   main conclusions on this set: (i) whether the model with the lowest power-fit RMSE also has the lowest
   injected-change error; (ii) whether the reference, the best two-stage model and κ-HYDRA-D stay within δ = 0.05 %/yr
   of each other (locations weighted equally).
