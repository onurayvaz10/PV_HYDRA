# Amendment 2 to the revision protocol (2 October 2026; the commit time is the record)

**P3, κ-HYDRA-D-24 implementation.** The first runs of the 24-month variant (two systems) anchored the trend at
mid-record while the correction g had learned the level of the first 24 months with ρ = 0. The missing level offset
made the single trend parameter overshoot an injected change (recovery ratio about 1.4 on DKASC 7). These runs are
withdrawn and kept only for the record (`results/fixed_mask_invalid_kappa24_anchor/`). In the corrected
implementation the trend is anchored at the centre c of the training window, P = P_PVW · g · [1 + ρ_w (t − t_mid − c)],
and expressed about mid-record as ρ = ρ_w / (1 − ρ_w c), the convention of the full-record fit. Because g is frozen,
the insolation-weighted pinball loss is convex in ρ_w, which is therefore found by a bounded scalar minimization on
all training hours instead of mini-batch gradient steps. The design of P3 (g trained on the first 24 data months with
ρ = 0, frozen, ρ fitted on the whole record) is unchanged. All P3 κ-HYDRA-D-24 results are produced with the corrected
implementation; a smoke test on two systems before the runs gave recovery ratios of 0.88–0.95, which are reported
with the full results whatever they show.
