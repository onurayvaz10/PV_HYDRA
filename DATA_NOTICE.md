# Data notice

No raw measurement data are included.

- DKASC data: download them with `scripts/fetch_dkasc.py` under the DKA Solar Centre terms, which forbid redistribution beyond 5,000 cells.
- PVDAQ (OEDI) and ERA5 (Open-Meteo): re-downloaded by the fetch scripts.

Result tables contain only per-system aggregates (rates, errors, scores).
